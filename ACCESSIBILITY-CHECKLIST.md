# Accessibility Checklist

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA

- Skip link present
- Semantic landmarks
- Keyboard-accessible dropdowns
- Escape-key menu close
- Visible focus states
- Accessible forms and labels
- Proposal errors announced through `aria-live`
- Reduced-motion video fallback
- Hero video pause control
- Descriptive alt text
- No horizontal overflow at mobile widths
- Touch targets at least practical mobile size
- Captions/transcripts required for production videos
