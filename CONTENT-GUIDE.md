# Content Guide

## Positioning

The website sells a premium, fully produced, high-impact experience that drives attendance and becomes the centerpiece of major events.

Use language around:

- Safety
- Insurance documentation
- Reliability
- Event logistics
- Vendor credibility
- Crowd appeal
- Attendance
- Operational support
- Contract value
- Planning simplicity
- Procurement support
- Brand impact

## Avoid

- Birthday-party positioning
- Cheap inflatable-rental language
- Unsupported technical claims
- Fake testimonials or fake event stats
- Thin SEO pages

## Pricing Language

Use:

`Experiences typically begin at $5,500, with large-scale activations ranging from $15,000 to $40,000+.`

Pricing disclaimer:

`Pricing varies by location, duration, travel, staffing, venue access, equipment selection, site conditions, permitting, and production requirements.`
