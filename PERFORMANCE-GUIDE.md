# Performance Guide

Targets:

- Lighthouse Performance: 90+
- Accessibility: 95+
- Best Practices: 95+
- SEO: 95+

Requirements:

- Replace placeholders with compressed WebP/AVIF images.
- Compress hero video and use lightweight mobile source.
- Keep video muted and preload metadata only.
- Use poster image fallback.
- Do not add slider or animation-library bloat.
- Put app behind CDN/cache where appropriate.
- Keep analytics deferred and consent-aware.
