Technical SEO included:

- Unique page titles
- Unique meta descriptions
- Canonical URLs
- Open Graph tags
- Twitter card tags
- XML sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`
- Robots file at `/robots.txt`
- Organization/EntertainmentBusiness schema
- FAQ schema where appropriate

Do not invent street address, hours, reviews, awards, coordinates or certifications.
